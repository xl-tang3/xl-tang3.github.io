Research Interests

My research interests lie in (multi-marginal) optimal transport & barycenters, and optimization methods, as well as their applications to address fundamental challenges in representation learning, multimodal understanding and generation, and image restoration. I am also interested in analyzing their theoretical properties and generalization behavior. In addition, my work places a strong emphasis on practical system implementation and validation in real-world scenarios.
